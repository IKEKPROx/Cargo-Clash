CARGO CLASH (Mac)
COMP4122 course project. A truck has crashed. Collectors carry the scattered cargo back to the
truck, and Interrupters smash it. A level has two halves (1 minute each in the tutorial, 3 minutes
on River Flats; 1 minute on Ruin Courtyard), and the sides swap at half-time.

RUNNING THE GAME
1. Unzip, and move CargoClash.app wherever you like (Applications, for example).
2. Double-click CargoClash.app. The app isn't signed by Apple, so the first time macOS says it can't
   check it for malware. Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
   the message about CargoClash and click Open Anyway, then Open. After that it opens normally.
   (Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine followed by the app, dragged into the window.)
3. To play alone, click PLAY, then PRACTICE WITH BOTS, then TUTORIAL, RIVER FLATS or MAYA RUINS. The
   YOUR CREW page opens: pick a body type, a hat, glasses and a face, then click START. No network
   or account is needed. The cards with a lock aren't ready yet.

SETTINGS
SETTINGS on the main menu, or Esc in a match and then SETTINGS: language (English or 简体中文),
camera, look speed, invert mouse Y, window and resolution (pick them, then click APPLY), VSync, a
frame rate limit, graphics (lower is faster) and volume. Settings are saved on this Mac.

CONTROLS
WASD                 Move
Mouse                Look around (the arrow keys turn too)
E                    Pick up / put down cargo
Space                Ram
Left-click           Attack: Collectors blast air, Interrupters swing a hammer (hold it on cargo to smash it)
Right-click          Collectors hold it to suck in cargo and people. Interrupters hold it to charge, and let go to slam.
F                    Inspect what's in your hands
V                    Switch between first person and over the shoulder
Esc                  Pause menu, with the settings

MAYA RUINS / 玛雅遗迹
The V09 ruins, trees and pits are included, with 8 value-10 boxes, 5 value-30 boxes and 2 value-100
boxes. The two 100 boxes are inside operable vaults and need two carriers.
Empty-handed, aim and press E to use gates, collect/throw vines, attach a vine ladder, or join a
teammate's boost. Interrupters hammer breakable ruins with left-click; Collectors hold right-click
to restore them. Ground vines slow players. Players and unscored cargo recover after falling into
a pit. Gates and restored walls wait until their opening is clear.

PLAYING TOGETHER ON A LOCAL NETWORK
One player clicks CREATE ROOM and chooses a level. The room code is that computer's IP address.
The others type the code and click JOIN. If macOS asks whether to accept incoming connections, click Allow.
A Mac and a Windows PC can play together.
Everyone needs a matching game build that includes the selected map.

REQUIREMENTS
A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later. Intel Macs can't run this build.
